About the brand

We make clothes.
You buy them.
Simple.

I'm a sucker started because someone had to. We make one thing: clothing that costs too much and says exactly that. No mission statement. No founder story about a trip to Bali. No charity tie-in. Just fabric with an honest price tag.

The name isn't an insult. It's a mirror. You know what you're doing. So do we. And somehow that makes it better.

We're building a small collection, slowly, without urgency. Because hype is exhausting and we'd rather just make good things.

I'm a sucker. Honesty. Reluctantly. Since now.

01

Transparent pricing.

It costs what it costs. No fake sales, no inflated "original" prices, no psychological games. Just a number.

02

No brand values.

We don't have a manifesto about saving the planet. We have a shirt. If that's enough for you, great.

03

Small by design.

We're not scaling. We're making a few things we like, selling them to people we assume are adults.

04

No FOMO.

We won't tell you it's limited edition. Maybe it is. Maybe it isn't. You'll live either way.
